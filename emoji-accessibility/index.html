Emoji Accessibility Reference

Every Unicode emoji and the text a screen reader will announce, side by side across platforms. Use it to spot emoji whose announced name differs between Apple VoiceOver, Windows readers and Android TalkBack — and to choose emoji whose meaning survives being read aloud.

Important: Actual output depends on the screen reader version, OS version, locale, and verbosity settings. The Windows column merges Narrator, NVDA, and JAWS; their announcements usually match but can diverge — check with the specific reader you target. Most emoji read identically across platforms; the value here is in spotting where they don't.
