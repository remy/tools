Isle of Man

Crown Dependency

The Isle of Man has one of the world's oldest parliaments — Tynwald, founded around 979 AD. It came under English then British control in the 15th century. It is a Crown Dependency with significant self-governance.

1765 (Revestment Act, full Crown control)

Key events

979
Tynwald is traditionally dated to around this year (the date is not documented; firm evidence begins in the 13th century). It is often described as the oldest continuous parliament in the world — a claim it shares with Iceland's Alþingi (930).
1765
Revestment Act — the British Crown purchases the island's rights from the Dukes of Atholl.

Atrocities & injustices

⚠ What the history books often omit

During WWI and WWII the island was used as an internment camp for 'enemy aliens' — German, Austrian, and Italian civilians including Jewish refugees fleeing Nazi persecution.

Today

The present

The Isle of Man is a low-tax jurisdiction and financial centre. It has its own government, currency, and laws, and is not part of the UK or EU.
