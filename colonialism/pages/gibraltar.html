Gibraltar

British Overseas Territory

Captured by Britain in 1704, Gibraltar is a strategically vital rocky promontory at the entrance to the Mediterranean. Spain continues to claim sovereignty. Gibraltarians have consistently voted to remain British, most recently in 1967 and 2002.

Key events

1704
British and Dutch forces capture Gibraltar during the War of the Spanish Succession.
1713
Treaty of Utrecht cedes Gibraltar to Britain in perpetuity.
1969
Spain closes the border with Gibraltar, which remains closed until 1985.
2016
Gibraltar votes 96% to Remain in the EU. Brexit creates significant friction with Spain.

Atrocities & injustices

⚠ What the history books often omit

The original Moorish and Spanish population was largely expelled after British takeover. In WWII, nearly 17,000 civilians were evacuated to Britain, Jamaica, and Madeira, living in poor conditions for several years.

Today

The present

Gibraltar has its own parliament and government. The economy relies on financial services, online gambling and tourism. Brexit created renewed Spanish pressure over sovereignty, and border arrangements remain a source of tension.
