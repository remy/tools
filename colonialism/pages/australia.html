Australia

Independent Commonwealth Realm

Australia was claimed by Britain in 1770 on the legal fiction of terra nullius — that it was uninhabited land. The continent had between 300,000 and 1 million indigenous inhabitants. Colonisation brought disease, dispossession, and massacres. Australia became a federation in 1901 and is now a Commonwealth Realm.

Key events

1770
James Cook claims eastern Australia for Britain under terra nullius.
1788
First Fleet arrives. Penal colony established at Sydney Cove.
1789
Smallpox epidemic devastates Aboriginal populations around Sydney, killing an estimated half of those near the harbour. Its origin remains debated.
1838
Myall Creek Massacre — 28 Aboriginal men, women and children murdered by settlers. Seven settlers hanged — one of the few times perpetrators were held accountable.
1869
Legislation begins giving colonial authorities power to remove Aboriginal children from families.
1901
Federation. The White Australia Policy formally begins.
2008
Prime Minister Kevin Rudd delivers formal apology to the Stolen Generations.

Atrocities & injustices

⚠ What the history books often omit

The Stolen Generations — Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children forcibly removed from their families by government authorities, mainly between about 1910 and 1970 (with removals beginning earlier) — is the defining atrocity of Australian colonialism. Estimates of the number removed vary widely, from around 20,000–25,000 up to 100,000; the official finding was that between one in ten and one in three Indigenous children were taken. The broader destruction of Aboriginal society through disease (sometimes deliberate), massacre, and dispossession reduced the indigenous population by an estimated 90% within decades of British arrival. A formal count of frontier massacres has documented over 400 massacre sites.

Today

The present

Australia is a wealthy G20 nation. Indigenous Australians remain significantly disadvantaged across virtually every measure — health, education, incarceration, life expectancy. A 2023 referendum on an Indigenous Voice to Parliament was defeated. The republic debate continues periodically. Australia is a close ally of Britain and the US.
