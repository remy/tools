Canada

Independent Commonwealth Realm

Canada was colonised by both France and Britain. After the Seven Years' War Britain took control. Canada became a dominion in 1867, gaining full constitutional independence in 1982. King Charles III remains head of state.

Atrocities & injustices

⚠ What the history books often omit

The residential schools system — in which an estimated 150,000 indigenous children were forcibly removed from their families and sent to church-run boarding schools — operated from the 1880s until the last federally-run school closed in 1997. Children were forbidden to speak their languages, subjected to physical and sexual abuse, and thousands died. From 2021, ground-penetrating radar surveys detected hundreds of soil anomalies believed to be unmarked graves at former school sites (no remains have yet been exhumed, and the careful term is 'probable/potential burials' rather than confirmed mass graves). A Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2015) described the system as cultural genocide.

Today

The present

Canada is a prosperous, multicultural G7 nation. Reconciliation with indigenous peoples is an ongoing national challenge. The country is a Commonwealth Realm, with ongoing debate about becoming a republic.
